# Suggested plans: where the ranges come from

Researched on **2026-11-26** from public listings. Every figure is a typical range for one person and an estimate, never a quote. Amounts in the catalog are in whole US dollars, converted at the ECB reference rate of 2026-11-26 (1 USD = 159.26 JPY, 0.91116 EUR). Check this note and `SUGGEST_RESEARCHED_ON` together whenever the catalog changes ([ADR-0020](../../architecture/adr/0020-suggested-plans-and-curated-cost-ranges.md)).

Items marked **approximate** had no single current listing. The range is a conservative judgement from similar listings; the owner reviews these first.

## Japan

- **Daily costs:**
  - Budget: hostel or capsule ¥3,000 to ¥9,500, food ¥3,000 to ¥6,000.
  - Comfort: business or mid-range hotel ¥10,000 to ¥30,000.
  - Sources: [JNTO budget guide](https://www.japan.travel/de/guide/japan-on-a-budget), [japan-guide.com](https://japan-guide.com/e/e2410.html), [LIVE JAPAN](https://livejapan.com/en/article-a0002298).
- **Legs:**
  - Tokyo to Kyoto Shinkansen reserved, about ¥13,650 to ¥14,500 ([Ekitan](https://ekitan.com/en/article/tokyo-to-kyoto-shinkansen), [Tokyo Cheapo](https://tokyocheapo.com/travel/transport/how-much-does-it-cost-to-ride-the-shinkansen)).
  - Hakone Free Pass from Shinjuku ¥7,100 for 2 days ([Odakyu](https://odakyu-global.com/passes/hako)).
- **teamLab Planets:** from ¥3,600 to about ¥4,600 by time slot ([teamLab](https://www.teamlab.art/en/e/planets/)).
- **Daikoku PA car meet tours:** ¥9,800 shared to ¥18,800 private ([LIVE JAPAN](https://livejapan.com/en/vt487762P1)).
- **Ebisu Circuit drift ride:** ¥16,550 to ¥25,000 per ride, plus the Fukushima train and a track fee ([Fukushima travel](https://fukushima.travel/tours/jumptour.php?id=22), [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/article/10927/team-orange-drift-taxi-takes-you-on-a-sideways-tour-around-ebisu-circuit)). One operator had paused bookings in 2025.
- **Hozugawa rafting:** ¥3,580 to ¥9,600 ([Activity Japan](https://en.activityjapan.com/feature/kyoto_hozugawa_rafting)).
- **Universal Studios Japan, 1 day:** from ¥8,400 to about ¥10,900 with date pricing ([WDWNT](https://wdwnt.com/2026/06/studio-pass-pricing-updated-at-universal-studios-japan-making-early-purchases-more-affordable/)).
- **Approximate:** the Golden Gai bar evening, Mount Takao, Shimokitazawa, the Hakone day bath, the Open-Air Museum, Pontocho, Kurama–Kibune, Shinsekai, Tenma and Minoo.

## Portugal

- **Daily costs:**
  - Budget: dorm €20 to €40, food €25 to €35.
  - Comfort: €120 to €210 a day.
  - Sources: [VIA Travelers](https://viatravelers.com/?p=257733), [travelwithhello](https://www.travelwithhello.com/blog/portugal-travel-budget-guide), [Budget Your Trip](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/Portugal).
- **Legs:** Lisbon to Porto train about €20 to €27 ([The World Was Here First](https://www.theworldwasherefirst.com/is-portugal-expensive/)).
- **Ericeira surf lesson:** €40 to €55 ([Expedia listings](https://www.expedia.ie/things-to-do/ericeira-surf-lesson.a52596459.activity-details)).
- **Pena Palace:** €14 to €22.40 by ticket type ([Parques de Sintra](https://www.parquesdesintra.pt/en/plan-your-visit/opening-times-and-prices/)).
- **Douro day trip:** about €86 to €113 shared ([GuruWalk](https://www.guruwalk.com/walks/p31063-douro-valley-and-wine-day-trip-from-porto-cGFub3B0ZXM)).
- **Approximate:** the fado evenings, the tasca, Bairro Alto, Arrábida kayak, the Joanina Library, the Mondego kayak, the port cellar, Livraria Lello, the francesinha, Galeria de Paris and Matosinhos surf.

## Argentina and Uruguay

- **Daily costs:**
  - Buenos Aires budget US$30 to US$80, mid-range US$70 to US$180.
  - Parrilla dinner US$15 to US$30.
  - Sources: [Hostelz](https://www.hostelz.com/en/backpacker-budget/cities/buenos-aires-argentina), [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/argentina-on-a-budget).
- **Tango dinner show:** about US$65 to US$75.
- **Ferry Buenos Aires to Colonia:** from about US$41, average about US$76 ([Openferry](https://openferry.com/en/routes/buenos-aires-colonia-del-sacramento)).
- **Tandem skydive near Buenos Aires:** about US$239 to US$338 ([Dicas de Viagem](https://www.dicasdeviagem.com/passeios/salto-de-paraquedas-em-chascomus/)).
- **Approximate:** the Colonia to Montevideo bus, Colonia and Montevideo daily costs, the milonga, Recoleta, San Telmo, the Tigre kayak, the Colonia bike ride and winery, Mercado del Puerto and the Rambla.
